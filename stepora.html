PRACTICE PROJECT

The Stepora House

Footwear Store / E-commerce Website

About This Project

The Stepora House is a modern footwear showroom and e-commerce website designed to provide a clean, attractive and user-friendly shopping experience.
The project focused on creating an engaging online presence for the footwear brand while keeping the shopping journey simple and convenient.
It also includes organized product categories and a responsive layout to provide a smooth browsing experience across different devices.

The website showcases different footwear categories and products with a simple and responsive layout.
